About

Born in London, acclaimed tenor Ben Johnson enjoys a varied career as a singer, conductor, teacher, and artistic director. He represented England in the BBC Cardiff Singer of the World 2013 and won the Audience Prize.

Much in demand in concert, recital, and opera, he has worked with some of the world's greatest conductors and has made multiple appearances at the BBC Proms.

Teaching

Ben is a highly regarded vocal coach and educator. He currently serves as a Professor of Singing at the Royal College of Music in London, helping to shape the next generation of professional vocal talent.

Festivals & Artistic Direction

In addition to his singing career, Ben is the Festival Director of the Cambridge Summer Music Festival and the founder/Chief Conductor of the Southrepps Sinfonia.
